02

Airline Sentiment Analysis

NLP · Transformers

Classified ~22,000 airline reviews into negative / neutral / positive sentiment, comparing classical baselines (TF-IDF + Logistic Regression, Naive Bayes) against a fine-tuned DistilBERT model. BERT lifted accuracy from 0.75 to 0.82, but neutral-class F1 barely moved (0.27 → 0.26) — the write-up argues that's a labeling-ambiguity ceiling, not a model-capacity problem.

Industrial Energy Cost Optimization

Cost Analytics · Time-of-Use Tariffs

Analyzed 15-minute interval electricity data from a steel manufacturer against Malaysia's Time-of-Use tariff structure. Found 81% of consumption fell in peak-rate hours against a load factor of just 0.17, with 277 demand spikes driving avoidable charges. Simulated load-shifting and demand-capping strategies projected RM 20,000–25,000 in annual savings.

Student Placement Analysis

EDA · Explainability

Explored what actually drives campus placement outcomes across 100,000 student records — academics, technical skills, and experience. Internship count turned out to be the strongest single factor, outweighing CGPA; a logistic regression baseline reached 0.69 ROC AUC, and the analysis is upfront that no single-feature or composite score cleanly separates the classes.
